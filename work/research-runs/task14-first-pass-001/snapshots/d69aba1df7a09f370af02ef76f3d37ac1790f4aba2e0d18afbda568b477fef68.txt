Notion MCP lets your AI assistant find answers in your workspace, turn notes into documents, and manage your projects in Notion. Connect an MCP client, such as Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
What you can do with Notion MCP
Notion MCP lets your AI assistant search your workspace, create and edit pages, and manage databases. Describe what you need, and your AI assistant can find the relevant pages in Notion.
