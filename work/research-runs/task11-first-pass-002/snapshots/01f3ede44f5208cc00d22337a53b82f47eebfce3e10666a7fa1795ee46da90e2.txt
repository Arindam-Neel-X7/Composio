FAQs
What is Shopify and how does it work?
Shopify is an all-in-one commerce platform that lets you create an online store, accept payments, and sell in person, on social channels, and in AI chats—all from one dashboard. Chat your store into existence or start from a theme, manage products and inventory, process orders and shipping, and let Sidekick, your very own commerce AI, take on marketing, analytics, and everyday admin—from web or mobile, no coding required.
What AI comes with Shopify?
Every plan includes Sidekick, your very own commerce AI, plus generous AI usage to build and run your store. Design your store by chatting with AI, connect the AI tools you already use, or build with Shopify CLI. And your products can show up in AI chats, ready for shoppers to buy.
How much does Shopify cost?
Shopify starts with a free 3-day trial—no credit card required. After your trial, most plans start at just ₹20 per month for the first 3 months, then switch to standard monthly pricing.
Additionally, you can choose to add POS Pro to your subscription for more advanced point of sale features like staff roles and permissions, exchanges, and flexible customer pickup and delivery options.
Can I cancel or change my subscription anytime?
Yes. If you ever decide that Shopify isn't the best commerce platform for your business, simply cancel your account.
If you choose a 1- or 3-year Plus subscription, you can cancel when your term commitment expires.
Are there any additional fees?
There are no setup fees on any of our plans.
Third-party transaction fees may apply when you use a third-party payment provider (instead of Shopify Payments). The rate depends on your plan.
Shopify charges a small fee to allow you to accept major credit cards, such as Visa and Mastercard, as payment in your store. These fees vary depending on the plan that you choose.
In what countries can I use Shopify?
Shopify is available in nearly every country in the world. Some features vary by country—for example, Shopify Payments is available only in certain countries—so pricing and payment options can differ depending on where your business is based.
Is Shopify PCI Compliant or PCI Certified?
Yes. Shopify is certified Level 1 PCI DSS compliant, and stores powered by Shopify are PCI compliant by default, which helps keep payment information secure.
Can I use my own domain name with Shopify?
Yes. You can use a domain you already own, or buy a new one. Every Shopify store also comes with a free myshopify.com address, and Shopify helps keep your site secure automatically.
If you already have a domain, you can keep it where you bought it and simply connect it to Shopify—or move it to Shopify so everything is managed in one place.
Do I get web hosting with Shopify?
Yes. Shopify includes secure commerce hosting with your Shopify plan, so you don't need to buy separate hosting. Shopify also includes unlimited bandwidth (no bandwidth fees) and provides a free TLS/SSL certificate for domains you add to Shopify.
Additionally, you can use the Buy Button sales channel to embed buy buttons on an external website or blog, and let customers check out using Shopify Checkout.

Shopify is a tech company, not a bank. We partner with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank N.A., Member FDIC. Shopify Balance Visa® Commercial cards are powered by Stripe and issued by Celtic Bank pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc. All funding through Shopify Capital in the U.S. is issued by WebBank. Shopify Credit Visa® Commercial Credit cards are powered by Stripe and issued by Celtic Bank pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc. See Issuing Bank Terms and Program Terms.
The Balance earnings rate is a reward provided by Shopify and is not interest. The rate is variable, subject to change without notice, and accurate as of November 19, 2026. The reward accrues daily and is paid monthly in the form of a credit to your Balance account. ACH limits may apply. See Reward Terms. Stores on Shopify Plus are rewarded with 3.54%, and all other plans receive 2.52%.
"Cashback" refers to rewards earned as a percentage discount on eligible purchases. Earn 3% cashback as a statement credit on up to US$250,000 of annual eligible purchases in your monthly top spend category—either marketing, fulfillment, or wholesale, and 1% cashback thereafter. Earn 1% cashback on the other two spend categories. Restrictions apply. See Program Terms for details. By default, you are opted in to paying your full statement balance by an automatic debit from your designated bank account within 1 month to avoid fees. You have the option to switch to making daily payments from your sales and take up to 10 months to pay your full statement balance, for a fee. If payment is not received in full within 10 months, a 2% monthly late fee will apply to your past-due balance.
Offers to apply do not guarantee funding. Shopify Capital loans are repaid based on a percentage of daily sales. The maximum repayment term is 18 months, and 2 minimum payments apply. The actual time period within which the loan is repaid may be less than 18 months. Funds through Shopify Capital are added to your account in as little as two business days, if approved.
