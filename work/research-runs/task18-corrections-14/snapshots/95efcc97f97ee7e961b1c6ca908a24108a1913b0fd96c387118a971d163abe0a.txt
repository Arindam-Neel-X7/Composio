Get started with monday MCP
Connect & automate
2 min read
monday MCP is the hosted server that gives AI tools secure access to your monday.com workspace. It connects your account directly to AI assistants like Claude, Cursor, and Copilot Studio through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for AI integrations.
Prerequisites
An active monday.com account with appropriate permissions
MCP is preinstalled on all monday.com accounts.
If you can't connect, ask your admin to check Admin → Permissions → AI Connectors. See Managing AI Connectors & MCP Access for more information.
Supported AI client
OAuth consent for monday MCP when prompted by your client
What can you do with monday MCP?
Project reporting – generate sprint summaries, team performance reports, and deadline tracking from your boards (including monday dev sprints)
Smart task management – convert meeting notes into structured items, assign tasks, and update statuses through natural language
Cross-team visibility – ask for rollups like “What’s blocking the launch?” across Product, Marketing, and RevOps boards
Operational workflows – create and triage incidents, support requests, and ops checklists (with clear owners, priority, and due dates)
CRM workflows – create leads and deals, update pipeline stages, and log next steps from call notes
Docs + knowledge workflows – create specs, SOPs, and retros as monday docs (in a workspace or attached to an item)
Dashboards & insights – build dashboards and widgets, and answer questions using board insights (such as status distribution, workload, and throughput)
Workspace discovery – quickly find the right board, doc, form, or folder, even in large workspaces
Key use cases
Quick data retrieval – ask for board status, find items, or summarize what changed this week
Task creation and cleanup – turn a meeting transcript into tasks with owners, priority, and due dates
Updating existing work – update status, people, or date fields when plans change, with human review before applying changes
Cross-board analysis – ask questions like “What’s overdue across all launch boards?” or “Which team has the most blockers?”
Executive reporting – create weekly rollups such as shipped vs. planned, scope changes, and risk summaries
Support and Service operations – intake requests, link relevant context, and keep stakeholders updated through item updates
Documentation workflows – generate a spec in monday docs and attach it to the relevant initiative item
Automation-style chains – run multi-step flows like “Create a bug, assign it, set severity, and notify the team in an update”
Note: Other tools that support the Model Context Protocol should also be compatible, though individual setup steps may vary

FAQs
Pricing and availability
Is MCP included in my monday.com plan?
Yes. MCP is available on all monday.com plans at no additional cost.
Are there usage limits?
Standard API limits apply.
Security and privacy
What permissions does MCP have?
Each user individually authorizes MCP via OAuth. MCP only has access to the data that you, as an individual user, can access in monday.com. The specific permissions depend on the OAuth scopes you grant during setup.
Is my data secure?
Yes. MCP uses industry-standard OAuth for authentication and TLS encryption for all data transfers. MCP respects your monday.com permissions, meaning you can only access or modify data you’re authorized to change. Because MCP can perform actions on your behalf, we recommend reviewing AI-suggested changes before confirming actions that modify your workspace.
Who can see my data?
Only you and your AI assistant can see the data exchanged through MCP. monday.com does not review or access the content of your conversations.
