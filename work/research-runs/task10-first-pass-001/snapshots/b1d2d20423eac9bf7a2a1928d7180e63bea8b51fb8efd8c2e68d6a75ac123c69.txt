HighLevel MCP Server
Stop clicking. Start asking.
Your next booking, follow-up, or invoice can start with a sentence. Connect HighLevel to your AI assistant, then ask it to find a contact, book an appointment, send a message, update a deal, or schedule a social post. The assistant works directly with your HighLevel account, so you can get work done from the conversation you’re already having.
More than 700 actions across 38 HighLevel features give you room to hand off everyday work:
Follow up while the context is fresh. Look up a lead’s messages, check their appointments, and send your reply from the same chat.
Turn a decision into action. Create and send an invoice, update a deal, or add a follow-up task without opening each record yourself.
Keep marketing moving. Schedule social posts, review email campaign results, and manage promotions.
Skip screen navigation. The assistant searches and updates records directly, without opening HighLevel pages or reading screenshots. See why use MCP.
Keep access on your terms. Connect the sub-accounts you want to use and approve the permissions. Agencies with multi-sub-account installation enabled can switch between connected sub-accounts in one chat.
No API key to create or paste. Sign in with your HighLevel login, connect your sub-account, and try your first request.
One request, from lookup to follow-up​
“Find open deals in the Sales Pipeline worth more than $5,000. Show me the largest first, then add a follow-up task for Friday to each contact.”
That request connects your pipeline to your follow-up work. The assistant can find the matching deals, use their linked contacts to create tasks, and report the results in your chat. You supply the goal; it handles the lookups and individual actions.
Hand off whole jobs​
You can ask for work that spans several parts of HighLevel:
Prepare for a customer conversation: “Catch me up on Jane Cooper: her latest messages, upcoming appointments, open deals, and unpaid invoices.”
Turn interest into a booking: “Find an open consultation slot next week, book Jane Cooper, then text her the appointment details.”
Finish the sale: “Create and send Jane Cooper an invoice for three coaching sessions at $150 each, then mark her deal as won.”
Launch a promotion: “Schedule these three posts on our connected Facebook account for Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9 a.m., and create a 20% coupon for the promotion.”
Build follow-up that keeps working: “Draft a welcome workflow for consultation requests: tag the contact, send a welcome SMS, wait one day, then create a follow-up task.”
The assistant combines supported actions to carry out the request. It may need details such as the calendar, recipient, or sending account; required permissions and existing product setup still apply. Each action runs separately, so review its results when a job has several steps.
Why use MCP instead of asking AI to click through HighLevel?​
Give your assistant a direct connection to the work. With MCP, it can ask HighLevel for records and act on the results without navigating menus, opening each contact, or scrolling through screens.
For a request like “Find Jane, book her consultation, and text her the details,” the assistant can look up her contact, check calendar availability, create the appointment, and send the message through HighLevel’s supported operations. That removes the page navigation between those steps.

More ways to put your assistant to work​
Beyond CRM updates, you can organize Projects and recurring tasks, manage Courses and enrollments, review affiliate commissions, maintain Media Storage, build Client Portal apps, and create Countdown Timers. You can also build and publish workflows, inspect their execution history, and compare failures by step.
Ask “What can you do with invoices?” to discover the actions available to your connection. As HighLevel adds supported actions, your assistant can discover them through the same connection. New permissions require fresh approval; some clients may need a new chat or tool refresh.

Working across sub-accounts (agencies)​
▶️ Walkthrough video: Multiple sub-account support in Claude.ai — watch on Loom
Connect a single sub-account for everyday work, or use an agency installation with the multi-sub-account option enabled to work across many connected sub-accounts from the same chat.
Install for your agency. When you connect, choose which sub-accounts to include: one, several, or all. The assistant can reach those sub-accounts and no others.
Name the sub-account. Refer to a sub-account by name, such as “in Downtown Clinic, …”. If the sub-account isn’t clear, name it before asking for a change.
Switch in the same chat. Ask about one sub-account, then another, without reconnecting: “Now do the same for Uptown Med Spa.”
“Compare how many new contacts Downtown Clinic and Uptown Med Spa added this month.”
Each action in this guide runs in one selected sub-account. Comparing two sub-accounts uses separate actions for each. Access uses the connection’s authorized sub-account credentials; an unauthorized sub-account is refused.
Availability: working across sub-accounts requires the multi-sub-account (bulk) installation option for your app. If your install only offers a single sub-account, connect that sub-account to get started.
Stay in control​
The assistant only gets the access you give it, and HighLevel checks that access every time it acts.
You choose the sub-accounts. The assistant can only work in the sub-accounts you selected when you connected.
You choose the permissions. It can only take actions your approved permissions allow, and each action is checked again when it runs.
No passwords or API keys to share. You sign in on HighLevel’s own sign-in page. Your assistant never sees your password.
Preview before anything changes. Ask the assistant to preview a change first. The server can return the proposed request without running that action. A preview does not simulate the result or confirm that HighLevel will accept the change.
Approve each change. Assistants that support approvals can ask you before every action that changes your data. Keep that setting on.
Workflows start as drafts. New workflows are saved as drafts, so nothing goes live until you publish. Review them in the workflow builder first.
Disconnect at any time. Remove the connection in your assistant, or revoke the app from your HighLevel account.
Actions affect your real account: sending a message initiates delivery, deleting a record can remove it permanently, publishing a workflow activates its triggers, and purchasing a phone number can incur charges. Try unfamiliar requests in a test sub-account first.
Set up your assistant​
Every assistant uses the same secure sign-in: sign in with your HighLevel login, choose your sub-account(s), and approve.
ChatGPT and Codex​
HighLevel is an official app in ChatGPT. Custom ChatGPT plugins, Codex, and other compatible OpenAI clients connect to https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/mcp/openai/v2/. This address ends with a slash.
ChatGPT​
Open HighLevel in ChatGPT Plugins and connect it.
Complete the sign-in (sign in → pick sub-account(s) → approve).
Start a new conversation, type @, and select HighLevel.
Or add HighLevel as a custom plugin:
Check that your account or workspace allows custom MCP connections.
Open ChatGPT Plugins, select the plus button, then Add custom MCP server.
Enter a name and description. Under Connection, add https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/mcp/openai/v2/ as the MCP server URL and complete the connection prompts.
Finish creating the plugin and complete the HighLevel sign-in (sign in → pick sub-account(s) → approve).
Start a new conversation, type @, and select HighLevel.
For current menu names and availability, see OpenAI’s custom MCP setup guide.

Codex​
Add the HighLevel server, then start the sign-in:
codex mcp add highlevel --url https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/mcp/openai/v2/
codex mcp login highlevel
In the Codex IDE extension, open the gear menu → MCP servers → Add server, choose Streamable HTTP, and enter the same URL. Save the server, select Restart extension, then Authenticate when sign-in is required. See the MCP setup guide.
Other compatible OpenAI clients can use the same address as a remote Streamable HTTP MCP server and complete the sign-in when prompted.

How it works​
HighLevel gives your assistant four tools to find and use the actions available to your connection. It can discover an action, read its requirements, and execute it without loading hundreds of separate action definitions upfront.
ToolWhat it does
list_locationsLists the sub-accounts this connection can use, so the right one is chosen
search_operationsFinds the right action for a request. It only returns actions your permissions allow
describe_operationShows what an action needs, with an example, the permissions it requires, and its safety information
execute_operationRuns the action after checking permissions and sub-account access, or previews it without sending anything
New supported actions can be discovered through the same tools. If an action needs a permission you haven’t approved, reconnect to grant it. Availability also depends on your sub-account’s enabled features.

“Find the contact with email [email protected], add the tag ‘vip-2026’, and create a $5,000 opportunity for them in the Sales Pipeline.”

Frequently asked questions​
What is MCP?​
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard for connecting AI assistants to the apps you use. HighLevel uses it to connect compatible assistants that support remote Streamable HTTP servers and HighLevel’s sign-in flow.
Can the assistant do everything I can do in HighLevel?​
It supports more than 700 actions across the features listed above. Coverage varies by feature: for example, it can read your published Agent Studio agents, while supported workflow actions include building and publishing. Ask what’s available for your connection, or tell us what’s missing.
Do I need to be technical?​
No. If you can describe what you want done in HighLevel, you can ask the assistant to do it. Setup takes a few clicks in ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Muse, or Grok Bot.
Do I need an API key or a Private Integration token?​
No. You connect by signing in with your HighLevel login, choosing your sub-account(s), and approving the requested permissions. There’s no key to create, paste, or rotate.
Will the assistant change things without asking me?​
Assistants that support approvals can ask before every action that changes your data, and new workflows are always saved as drafts. You can also ask for a preview of any change first. Keep approvals on, especially when you’re trying something new.
Can I undo a change?​
Not automatically. Treat changes as final: deleted contacts, records, and workflows can’t be restored through the assistant. Ask for a preview first, and try new kinds of requests in a test sub-account.
The assistant says it can’t do something, or reports a permission error.​
Check the error first. A missing-permission error may require disconnecting and reconnecting to approve new permissions; refreshing the connection doesn’t expand its existing grants. An action can also be unavailable because the sub-account isn’t authorized, the feature isn’t enabled, or a required service isn’t connected.
Can the assistant upload a file I attached in the chat?​
It depends on your assistant. If it can securely supply the original file, MCP supports uploading it to Media Storage, up to 25 MB per file; your assistant may have a lower limit. Chat attachments are not automatically passed to HighLevel. If your assistant cannot transfer the file, it can provide a link to the selected sub-account’s Media Storage page for you to upload it. It can also add an existing public HTTPS file by link; that mode keeps the file externally hosted.
Is this the same as the Developer MCP Server?​
No. This server connects AI assistants to the data in your HighLevel sub-accounts. The Marketplace Developer MCP Server is a separate tool that helps developers build and release Marketplace apps.
