Apify runs customer code and handles scraped data at scale. Platform security is built around strict tenant isolation, protection of your secrets and data, and a hardened software supply chain.

Apify's compliance posture and audit reports are covered below.
SOC 2 Type II compliance
The Apify platform is SOC 2 Type II compliant. An independent audit verified that Apify's information security practices, policies, procedures, and operations comply with SOC 2 standards for security, availability, and confidentiality of customer data.
Read the SOC 2 compliance announcement, or visit the Trust Center to request the SOC 2 Type II report.
