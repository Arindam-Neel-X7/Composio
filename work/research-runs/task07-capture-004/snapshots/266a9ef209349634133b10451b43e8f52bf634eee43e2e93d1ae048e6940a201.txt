CRM pricing frequently asked questions
How much does CRM cost?
CRM pricing varies based on your needs. Salesforce’s CRM is scalable so that you only have to pay for the features you use — and you can add or remove them as your needs change. You can find product-specific information and pricing here.
What are the different pricing plans available for Salesforce CRM?
Salesforce offers several pricing plans to cater to different business needs. These include Starter Suite and Pro Suite, Enterprise, and Unlimited. Each plan comes with a unique set of features and capabilities to help you manage and strengthen your customer relationships.
Can I try Salesforce CRM before committing to a plan?
Yes. Salesforce offers a free trial of our CRM solution so that you can explore all of the features and functionalities before making a purchase decision. Simply sign up for your free 30-day trial. Then use the guided onboarding feature to get familiar with the platform.
What is included in the Starter Suite plan?
Starter Suite is a complete CRM suite designed for small businesses that brings marketing, sales, service, and commerce tools together in one place. With Starter Suite, you can organize data, manage customer relationships, and gain insights into your business with one simple solution. Watch the demo video to see Starter Suite in action.
What is included in the Pro Suite plan?
Pro Suite takes the complete CRM suite to the next level. It builds upon Starter Suite with even more marketing, sales, service, and commerce tools in a single app. Pro Suite helps you manage your unique business processes with upgraded features, like customization and automation, as well as additional apps that support your needs. Watch the demo video to see Pro Suite features in action.
I’m an existing customer. How do I activate Foundations?
Enterprise customers and above can activate Foundations for free directly in ‘Your Account’ to add key features in Agentforce, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, and Commerce Cloud - directly in your existing CRM. With Foundations you can start using Agentforce for free - with 1,000 conversations to get started.
Are there any additional costs for add-ons or integrations?
Salesforce offers various add-ons and integrations, some of which may require additional costs. These include advanced analytics, marketing automation, and third-party integrations. It's important to review the pricing details for these add-ons to understand the total cost of your CRM solution.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?
Yes. Salesforce is built to scale as your needs change. You can add products or upgrade to different plans, though some products may need to scale together. Reach out to our sales department at 18006649073 to see what works for your business.
