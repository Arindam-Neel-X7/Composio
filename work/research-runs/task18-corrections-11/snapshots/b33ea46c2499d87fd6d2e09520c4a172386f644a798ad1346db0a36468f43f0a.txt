Get address book

https://api.squarespace.com

The default shipping address (when present) is always included on the first page of results. Requires OAuth website scope website.contacts.read or website.contacts, or API key scope CONTACT_READONLY or CONTACT, or OAuth website scope website.profiles.read or website.profiles (OAuth only).
